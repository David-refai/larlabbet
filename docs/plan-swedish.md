# Plan: Svenska in Lärlabbet (grades 4–9)

## Goal

The goal is a Swedish course that really teaches the language, not only the school subject. It is built for children who are learning Swedish alongside another home language, Arabic for example. It follows Lgr22 Svenska and Svenska som andraspråk (SvA): reading, writing, vocabulary, grammar, and narrative and factual texts. It adds what those courses lack in practice:
- a planned vocabulary that keeps coming back;
- grammar taught visually;
- questions that say clearly what they ask;
- help in English or Arabic when a word or rule is hard.

## One unit (about 20–30 minutes, split into short parts)

1. **Berättelsen (the story).** A short illustrated story or factual text, 120–400 words depending on the grade.
   - The text is shown in short pieces next to a picture.
   - Every new word is tappable. Tapping shows the meaning in simple Swedish, a synonym, an example, and the word in English and Arabic.
   - The story is written so that about a third of its words and one earlier grammar pattern come from earlier units. That review happens naturally inside the story; nothing tells the pupil that "this is a repetition".
2. **Nya ord (new words).** 6–10 words per unit, each with:
   - a picture or icon, a simple explanation and a synonym or opposite;
   - an example sentence from the story;
   - for nouns: the word form (en/ett) and the plural;
   - for verbs: the tense forms.

   Words go into the same memory system as maths: review after 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days, the "🧠 Sitter" tag when a word is mastered, and daily review.
3. **Grammatik (grammar).** One point per unit, drawn on the whiteboard like the maths lessons. Examples:
   - colours and arrows for word order: Igår **åkte** jag … (V2);
   - en/ett with an ending table;
   - verb tenses on a timeline;
   - adjective agreement: en röd bil, ett rött hus, röda bilar.

   It always uses sentences from the story. A short line in English or Arabic appears only where a rule really differs from the home language.
4. **Förstå texten (understanding the text).** 5–6 questions with clear wording.
   - Each question says exactly what to do: "Skriv ett ord från texten som betyder …", "Vem …?", "Varför tror du …?".
   - The test words (sammanfatta, motivera, jämför, synonym, motsats, huvudperson) get the same 📖 word chips as in maths, so pupils learn the words of the questions too.
5. **Öva (practice).** It mixes:
   - word questions (meaning, synonym, opposite, fill-in-the-gap);
   - grammar questions (pick or write the right form: en/ett, plural, tense);
   - one or two questions from earlier units.
6. **Skriv (write).** A short writing task: 2–5 sentences using 3 of the new words. In the Claude version, Claude gives kind feedback on spelling and grammar. On the public site, the pupil checks against a model text with a checklist.

## Content per grade (a first sketch, adjusted to Lgr22)

| Grade | Texts | Grammar thread |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | everyday stories, animals, school | en/ett, plural, present/past tense, capital letter and full stop, questions |
| 5 | adventure, simple fact texts | adjectives and agreement, V2 word order, linking words (och, men, för, så) |
| 6 | myths, letters, instructions | perfect tense, possessives, subordinate clauses (att, som, när, eftersom) |
| 7 | short stories, news, debate | word classes, synonyms and nuance, word formation (compounds, prefixes) |
| 8 | crime and science fiction, explanatory texts | passive voice, formal versus informal language, cohesion and paragraphs |
| 9 | novel extracts, argumentative texts, reports | argumentative structure, source criticism, style and register |

Each grade has about 12 units. With 6 grades that is about 72 units, built grade by grade like maths.

## What the app needs (engine)

- A story reader with tappable words, a picture per part and a progress bar.
- A word list per unit plus a global word bank, linked to the spaced review the app already has.
- Grammar scenes on the existing whiteboard, with coloured parts of the sentence.
- A new question type that takes a short word answer, with tolerant checking (case and small spelling slips noted).
- The same parent page, extended with Swedish: words that have stuck and words to practise.

## Order of work

1. Build the engine plus 2 pilot units for grade 7. David reviews them with a pupil.
2. Adjust the format from that feedback.
3. Write the rest grade by grade (4 workers in parallel, as with maths).
