# Svenska årskurs 7: the 12 units

Every unit follows sv7a/sv7b in `src/lessons/sv7.js`. Each has:
- a story of 7 parts and 300–400 words;
- 8 new words;
- one grammar point drawn on the whiteboard in about 3 scenes;
- 8–9 reading questions, including 2 that need inference;
- a `gram(lv,write)` generator (choice at levels 0–1, written answer at level 2).

Amir and Sara (and their class 7B, teacher Lena, coach, mum) run through all units, so the year reads like one book. Each story reuses at least 4 words from earlier units without saying so. Keys below are the TERM keys; they must stay unique.
