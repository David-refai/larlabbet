# Maths plan for Years 6–9 (Lgr22)

The lessons are listed in teaching order, and the ids match `COURSES` in `app/src/legacy/core.js`. Each group of four is written by one worker into `wip/y<year><letter>.js`.

## Year 6 (centralt innehåll 4–6, end of stage)
- **y6a**
  - prime6: divisibility rules, factors, primes, prime factorisation (factor tree)
  - order6: order of operations and brackets
  - negcalc6: adding and subtracting negative numbers (temperature, debts, number line)
  - decmd6: multiplying and dividing decimals (prices, by 10/100/1000, decimal × whole)
- **y6b**
  - fracadd6: adding and subtracting fractions with different denominators (common denominator)
  - mixed6: mixed numbers and fraction × whole number
  - pct6: percent, fraction and decimal as the same share (conversion, pie and 100-grid)
  - ratio6: proportionality: unit price, comparing prices, scaling a recipe
- **y6c**
  - scale6: scale on maps and drawings (1:100, 1:50 000, 5:1)
  - tri6: area of triangle and parallelogram (b·h/2, cutting and moving)
  - circle6: the circle: radius, diameter, circumference ≈ 3.14·d
  - vol6: volume of a cuboid; cm³, dm³ and litres
- **y6d**
  - eq6: equations with a balance scale (one step, then two steps)
  - pattern6: patterns and sequences: find the rule, the 10th figure
  - median6: mode, median and mean, and choosing the right one
  - prog6: algorithms and programming: step by step, loops, finding the bug (read a block program and give its output)

## Year 7 (centralt innehåll 7–9, first year)
- **y7a**
  - int7: multiplying and dividing negative numbers (sign rules)
  - pow7: powers and powers of ten (2⁵, 10⁴, square numbers)
  - sci7: scientific notation for big numbers (space distances, population)
  - round7: rounding, significant figures and estimating
- **y7b**
  - frac7: multiplying and dividing fractions
  - pct7: percent: part, whole and percentage (find any one of the three)
  - alg7: algebraic expressions: write, simplify, substitute
  - eq7: equations with x on both sides
- **y7c**
  - ang7: angles: supplementary, vertical, triangle angle sum, parallel lines
  - circ7: area and circumference of a circle with π
  - units7: area and volume units: cm², dm², m², cm³, dm³, litre conversions
  - comp7: composite shapes: area and perimeter
- **y7d**
  - prop7: proportionality: table, graph y = k·x, the constant k
  - stat7: diagrams: bar, line and pie charts; reading and choosing
  - prob7: probability and sample space: two dice, table of outcomes
  - prog7: programming: variables, conditions (if/else) and loops; trace the program

## Year 8
- **y8a**
  - pow8: laws of exponents (multiply, divide, power of a power, a⁰)
  - root8: squares and square roots (estimating √)
  - sci8: scientific notation for small numbers and prefixes (milli, micro, nano)
  - speed8: speed, distance and time; compound units (km/h ↔ m/s)
- **y8b**
  - pct8: growth factor: sales and increases, percentage points
  - interest8: interest, loans and saving (personal finance, compound interest over a few years)
  - paren8: brackets: expanding and factorising (common factor)
  - eq8: equations with brackets and fractions
- **y8c**
  - pyth8: Pythagoras' theorem
  - vol8: volume of prism and cylinder
  - sim8: similarity and scale factor (similar triangles, shadows)
  - poly8: angle sum of polygons, (n−2)·180°
- **y8d**
  - lin8: linear functions y = kx + m: slope and intercept, read from a graph
  - seq8: sequences: formula for the nth term (arithmetic)
  - spread8: averages and spread: range, quartiles, box plot reading
  - tree8: probability in several steps: tree diagrams

## Year 9
- **y9a**
  - quad9: squaring rules and the difference-of-squares rule
  - qeq9: quadratic equations: x² = a, factorised form (x−a)(x−b)=0, pq or completing the square at a simple level
  - formula9: formulas: substitute and rearrange to solve for a variable
  - sys9: simultaneous equations: substitution and graphical solution
- **y9b**
  - line9: equation of a straight line through two points
  - nonlin9: non-linear functions: y = x², y = k/x (inverse proportionality)
  - exp9: exponential growth and decay (population, value loss, half-life)
  - pct9: percent in several steps, change factor chains, back-calculation (price before VAT/sale)
- **y9c**
  - cone9: volume of cone, pyramid and sphere
  - surf9: surface area of cuboid and cylinder
  - scale9: area scale and volume scale (k² and k³)
  - pythapp9: Pythagoras in real life: diagonals, distance between points in a coordinate system
- **y9d**
  - comb9: combinatorics: how many ways (multiplication principle, codes, outfits)
  - prob9: probability with and without replacement
  - crit9: critical statistics: misleading diagrams, samples and conclusions
  - prog9: programming: functions and loops to simulate or compute (trace and predict output)
