# Svenska årskurs 4–6: 36 units

Each unit uses the typed format in `app/src/svenska/unit.ts` (`defineUnit`, `word`), one file per group of units in `app/src/lessons/sv4a.ts` …, and the same parts as Year 7: a story with tappable words, 8 new words, one grammar point drawn on the whiteboard in about 3 scenes, reading questions, and a `gram(lv, write)` generator.

**Lengths**
- Year 4: 150–200 words, 5 parts, 6 reading questions (1 inference).
- Year 5: 200–250 words, 6 parts, 7 reading questions (2 inference).
- Year 6: 300–400 words, 7 parts, 8 reading questions (2 inference).

**Language level**
- Year 4: short sentences, present and simple past, everyday words.
- Each year a little longer and richer.
- `d` (meaning) uses words simpler than the word itself. English and Arabic help in every `say`.

**Story world:** Björkskolan in Hagaby (the same school as Year 7). The main characters are Yasmin (Amir's little sister) and her friend Leo, with Leo's dog Kexi.
- Year 4: class 4A, teacher Karin.
- Year 5: 5A, the same teacher.
- Year 6: 6A, teacher Johan.

Grandparents, a neighbour and classmates (Elsa, Noah, Ibrahim) can appear. Fact texts, myths and instructions keep a short frame with the characters.

**Reuse:** each story reuses at least 4 words from earlier units (earlier years count), without saying so.
